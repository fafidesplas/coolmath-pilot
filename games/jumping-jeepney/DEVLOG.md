## 2026-10-17 — Prototype: runner, stops, billboards, power lines
- First playable: the jeepney drives right on its own, one button to jump and double jump with booster flames, 240×135 chunky pixel art.
- Riders wave on roads and roofs; their shirt color is their stop. Land on the matching rooftop to drop them off, pass it and they bail. Canals end the run.
- Floating billboards with made-up food brands; stops became a waiting shed plus a transit sign; riders call "PARA PO!" as their stop comes up.
- Live power lines from 150 m: drive under them over the road, hop them between roofs; a zap knocks the jeepney down and one rider bails.
