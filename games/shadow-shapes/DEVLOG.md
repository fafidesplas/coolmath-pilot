## 2026-10-28 — Phase 2a build plan
Fafi said go ahead without waiting for a plan review, so this is the plan the slice is built from.
- **Files** (STANDARDS.md layout, GDD 12): `index.html` (three stacked canvases: 2D background and wall, WebGL object view over the object zone, 2D UI on top that alone gets pointer events), `style.css`; `lib/three.module.min.js` (Three.js r170, pinned, with its `LICENSE`); `src/` = `main.js`, `config.js` (every tuning number), `pose.js` (dials → matrix, wrap, key steps, Spin stops, nearest solution, drag axis pick and the upside-down flip; pure), `shadow.js` (prism geometry, projection, hulls, 64×64 rasterization, IoU, picture outline, match %; pure), `sculpt.js` (picture parser, seeded PRNG, picture → connected blocks; pure), `level.js` (level module → pivot, mask frame, target, wall mapping), `scene3d.js` (Three.js orthographic camera, one merged mesh without internal faces, rings, camera swing; a Canvas 2D painter's fallback if WebGL is missing), `wall.js` (room, beam, wall, shadow, gap glow, spill hatching, outline, mosaic), `game.js` (loop, screens, drag and keys, snap and reveal, HUD, book, analytics glue), `ui.js` (text, buttons, icons), `fx.js`, `audio.js`, `save.js`, `analytics.js`, `input.js`. `levels/` = one module per level plus `index.js`. `tools/` = `simtest.mjs`, `levelcheck.mjs` (GDD 6 rules; `--write` builds the sculpture from the seed, retries seeds until the rules pass, and fills `blocks`, `match`, `base`, `solutions`).
- **Copied, not imported**, from Ink Path and Flip Runner: frame loop and letterbox, keyed canvas buttons (matched by key on pointerup), panels and menu keyboard focus, the FX pool, the synth and music sequencer, save and the GA stub, the simtest harness. Renamed and re-themed to Night Neon.
- **Order:** pure modules and `simtest.mjs` first; then pictures for levels 1–3 and `levelcheck.mjs` until all three pass; then the play screen (3D view, wall, drag, keys, snap, reveal); then the shell, audio and juice; last a throwaway Node smoke test of the whole game with a stubbed DOM and a stubbed WebGL renderer.
- **Faked or left out:** hint and ghost, Spin wheel, two parts, two lights, daily (its tab and button show locked), the editor, `sculpt.mjs` and `dailycheck.mjs` (sculpting lives in `levelcheck.mjs --write` for now), the title's logo art (drawn text with a cast shadow), the picture flying into the book (a pop instead).
- **Risks:** the drag feel on a trackpad (can only be judged by a person); WebGL on Chromebooks (painter's fallback ready, fps still to be measured); the level rules (proved in Node before any art).

- Wrote `GDD.md` from the brief using the recommended phase 1a answers. Key calls: three path-independent dials (Turn, Tip, Spin) with axis-locked drags and rings that show the axis before it turns; match by IoU on 64×64 masks from our own orthographic projection, with a threshold calibrated per level for a ±6° window; an honest match % plus spill and gap visuals on the wall; time stars with a hint cap; sculptures generated from pixel pictures and revealed as a glowing mosaic; rooms add Spin (Museum), two-part puppets (Theater) and two lights (Observatory).
