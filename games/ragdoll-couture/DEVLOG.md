# Ragdoll Couture — devlog

- Moved from the prototype-city repo with its git history; added this brief, the mechanic diagram and this devlog.

## 2026-10-17 — Aimed launches, idle poses, accessories, lenses, filters
- Drag-to-aim launches with a flight preview and power bar; a quick click or Space is still a plain toss.
- Jump training: the model jumps back up after landing for more shots. Idle poses between tosses; get-up time halved.
- Each model has their own pronouns, used in all text about them.
- New Accessories tab (hat, scarf, bag), camera lenses (wide, telephoto, fisheye) and a Filters tab of color grades on the print; photos render once offscreen.
- Pose scoring no longer rewards which way up the model is; shop tabs in two rows of three.

## 2026-10-16 — First prototype
- Fashion photography with a ragdoll: fling or toss the model, snap the photo mid-air; editors grade F to A on framing, pose, airtime and sharpness plus style, and pay by grade.
- Verlet stickman ragdoll drawn in round, outlined canvas strokes.
- Shop: wardrobe, models (flexibility), camera (shutter, burst, slow-mo, autofocus), studio (backdrops, lights, props, fan, trampoline).
- Cushions line the floor for soft landings.
