# Echo Steps — devlog

## 2026-10-27 — Vertical slice, first playable
- Built: deterministic 120 Hz room sim with no DOM (`src/sim.js`, `physics.js`, `tape.js`): run, variable jump, coyote, buffer, 6 px corner and lip nudges; loop clock that waits for the first move; Loop (E/Enter), Delete (Backspace), Restart (R); echoes replay their tape position by position, rest at their last spot (and drop if it ended mid-air), are one-way 32 px steps while standing, carry riders, never launch them; slots and "slots full"; plates and gates (AND and inverted supported in data); breaks when a gate closes in an echo's path. Forecast pre-run (`forecast.js`) drives the echo paths (next second bright, ▼ plate and ‖ rest marks), the gate countdown arcs and the timeline lanes.
- Rooms 1–3 (Echo, Step, Loop Now) as data in `levels/`, with cues (silhouettes, ← → / SPACE keycaps, E keycap on plates in room 3) and reference solutions in `levels/solutions/` (made by `tools/make-solutions.mjs`). Pars: 1 echo / 4 s, 1 / 5 s, 2 / 5 s.
- Shell: title (one-click PLAY, ROOMS, locked TRAILS, sound and music toggles, little demo), room select (3 world tabs, 12 tiles, stars, best echoes and time, next room bouncing), HUD (room, timeline from room 3, slots, goals with running room time, Loop / Delete / Restart / Pause buttons with ≥48 CSS px hit areas), pause (resume, restart, paths, music, sound, controls card, menu), 400 ms rewind with scanlines, exit stream, results with stamped stars, count-up and the winning loop replaying behind the panel at 1.5×, fast-forward (hold ↓/S), touch buttons in CSS px, saving, synthesized SFX and echo-layered music (on by default), GameAnalytics stub, `window.game`. Debug: `?room=w1l03`, `?replay`, `?record`, `?hz=144`.
- Verified in Node only (no browser or port in the agent sandbox): `node tools/simtest.mjs` passes 77 checks (jump apex 105 px, air time, tap hop, the whole climb table incl. the misses, gaps, coyote and buffer, exact echo replay, rest and drop, empty-tape echo, Delete, slots, room time, one-way echo tops, riding, gate timings, push-out, breaks, forecast; per room: solution reaches the exit, echo par, time par, determinism, 0.25 s tolerance on every loop, short waits, lone-runner bot fails, plate rule). A throwaway smoke test loaded `src/main.js` with a stubbed DOM and drove the real frame loop with key presses and real pointerdown/pointerup on button coordinates: 46 checks passed (title → PLAY → room 1 by keyboard → results → NEXT, pause and settings by mouse, HUD Loop/Delete by mouse, R, rooms 2 and 3 completed by replay, room 3 live, slots full, fast-forward, touch, hidden tab, broken save, resize, every GA id). `?replay` also completes all rooms at 30, 60 and 144 Hz.
- Left for later: hints and skip, trails, zap/laser and every Archive/Core element, `?edit`, `?forecast`, real GameAnalytics SDK. Not yet seen in a real browser.

## 2026-10-26 — GDD first draft and review
- Wrote `GDD.md` from the brief, using the recommended answers to the phase 1a questions. Key calls: echoes replay their recorded path exactly and break visibly if you change their past; a resting echo keeps its spot; the clock waits for your first move; a laser zap turns your attempt into an echo that blocks the beam; each stacked echo is worth one tile of climb (105 px jump).
- Previews are part of the design from the start (lesson from Magnet Twins): echo paths with the next second highlighted, forecast arcs on gates 1 s before echoes change them, and a timeline of echo events. Pace is designed in too (lesson from Flip Runner): 5–10 s loops, Loop key, fast-forward while waiting, music that gains a layer per echo.
- Phase 1c review applied: rooms 3, 10 and 26 fixed (two had a cheap solution, one was jumpable), no boost jumps off echoes, no floating echoes, a 0.25 s timing tolerance test and a lone-runner bot, hints from the reference solutions plus a skip. Full list in the GDD changelog. Status: In review.
