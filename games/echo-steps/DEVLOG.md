# Echo Steps — devlog

## 2026-11-17 — Hints and Skip room
- **Hints (GDD 7):** after 60 s of room time or 8 loops a lightbulb button appears in the HUD (left, after the room name; it pulses until the first hint is used). Also key **H** and a Hint entry in the Pause menu (greyed with a "HINT IN 0:42" countdown before that). Hint *k* draws echo *k* of the room's reference solution as a pale (#EAF0FF) dotted path with a "?k" ghost walking it on a loop, a ▼ where it presses a plate, a ‖ where it rests and an **E** keycap when that loop was ended early ("E before moving" for room 10's spawn step). The last hint draws the live route of the winning loop with a solid "?" ghost. All shown ghosts share one clock, so their timing matches the solution. Each hint sends `ui:hint` with its number.
- Hint paths are not hand-drawn: new `src/hints.js` fetches `levels/solutions/<id>.json`, replays it through the real sim and takes each echo's whole loop from the forecast of the winning loop (rests and drops included), plus the live route. If the file can't load, there's simply no bulb.
- **Skip room:** after 3 minutes of room time or once every hint has been shown, the Pause menu offers **SKIP ROOM ↷**. It sends `Fail` and `ui:skip`, saves `{ stars: [0,0,0], skipped: true }` in `cm_echo-steps_save`, unlocks the next room and starts it. The room select shows a ↷ mark and "skipped" on that tile until it is finished (finishing clears it and saves stars as usual). PLAY goes to the first room that is neither finished nor skipped. A finished room never offers skip.
- The thresholds keep counting through R (only entering another room, finishing or the menu resets them), so a stuck player who restarts still gets the bulb. Clarified in the GDD (7 and changelog).
- **Demo param:** `?hints=now` makes hints and skip available at once, e.g. `/games/echo-steps/?room=w1l07&hints=now`. It also works from the title (`?hints=now` alone). Not shown anywhere in the UI.
- Tests: `node tools/simtest.mjs` → **all 212 checks pass** (the 147 from before plus 65 new: per room, hint count = echo par + 1, each echo's hint path equals what the live player did in that loop step for step, and what the echoes did in the winning loop, the last hint equals the winning live route and ends in the exit; room 10's spawn step; the 60 s / 8 loops / 3 min / all-hints thresholds; the skip save shape). New `node tools/smoketest.mjs` drives the real game with a stubbed DOM → **all 41 checks pass** (demo param, bulb click, H, pause Hint/Skip, `ui:hint`/`ui:skip`/`Fail` events, the saved record, room unlocks and PLAY after a skip, 8 loops and 60 s unlocks in real frames, state across R, missing solution file, and every room 1–10 still finishing via `?replay`).

## 2026-11-16 — Lab rooms 7–10 (Let Go, Two Jobs, High Plate, Spawn Step)
- Added rooms 7 **Let Go** (loop 8 s, par 1 echo / 7 s), 8 **Two Jobs** (loop 8 s, 2 slots, par 1 / 8 s), 9 **High Plate** (loop 8 s, par 2 / 7 s) and 10 **Spawn Step** (loop 6 s, par 2 / 6 s) as in GDD 6. They're registered in `levels/index.js`, so they show in the room select, follow room 6 via NEXT and open with `?room=w1l07` … `w1l10` (`?replay&room=w1l07` plays the reference solution). Only existing mechanics are used: the inverted gate (already in the sim) in Let Go, and plates on a shelf in High Plate. No sim changes and no new cues (the GDD asks for none in 7–10).
- How to play them: **Let Go**: your echo holds plate ▲ until you're past gate ▲, then steps off left so the inverted gate ▲̸ opens; wait in the 4-tile room between them. If the echo never lets go, you're stuck between the gates. **Two Jobs**: one echo holds ▲ until you pass gate ▲, then walks to ● and rests (two echoes is the easy 2-star answer). **High Plate**: echo 1 rests at the foot of the 3-tile shelf, echo 2 climbs it and rests on the plate. **Spawn Step**: press E before moving (an echo rests right at the spawn as a step), then record one echo on the plate; climb and run. Both orders work.
- GDD changes (section 6 and changelog): Let Go's spawn moved from col 10 to col 8. With the spawn at col 10, an echo loop 0.25 s late left you bumping a shut gate ▲, which broke the tolerance rule. High Plate's and Spawn Step's plate-to-gate distances were corrected to the built layouts (17 and 18 tiles).
- Reference solutions are scripted in `tools/make-solutions.mjs` and written to `levels/solutions/`. A new `waitAt` helper makes the final loop stand still until a gate an echo opens is fully open, plus 0.35 s. That way the input stream still works when the echo's loop is delayed by 0.25 s.
- `node tools/simtest.mjs`: all 147 checks pass. That's the 107 from before plus the 10 room rules for each new room: exit, echo par, time par, determinism, solution file, loop replay, 0.25 s tolerance, short waits, lone-runner bot fails and plate rule. A throwaway Node check also confirmed that the other Spawn Step order and the 2-echo Two Jobs both solve, and that in Let Go an echo that never lets go blocks you. Rooms 7–10 pre-render with a stubbed canvas. They haven't been seen in a real browser yet.
