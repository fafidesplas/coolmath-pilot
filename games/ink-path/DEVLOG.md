# Ink Path — devlog

## 2026-10-27 — Vertical slice, first playable
- **Built:** deterministic 120 Hz sim (`src/sim.js`, no DOM, only + − × ÷ and sqrt) with the GDD contact rules: no bounce on ink, walls bounce 0.3, glancing hits under 25° keep speed, stroke ends and convex corners up to 10 px roll over at 95%, rolling resistance 60 px/s²; cup capture; fail rules lost / stuck (box and turn-back) / loop / timeout. The pen (`src/pen.js`): 4 px lazy string, a point every 6 px, coalesced pointer events, tip stops at blocked areas with a red tether, out-of-ink stop, RDP 1.5 px on release, lines under 20 px ignored.
- **Attempt flow:** let go → ink sets → trapdoor opens after 150 ms; drop preview; ghost of the last line; drawing again (drag > 6 px) abandons a run at once; R / ↻ resets, Z / Backspace cancels; fail bubble ("Oops!", "Stuck!", "Dizzy!", "Too slow!", "So close!") then an automatic reset; hint (💡 / H) after 3 real fails on levels 2–3; level 1's dashed guide and ghost hand; level 2's bouncing meter; level 3's bouncing star notches with "Less ink, more stars".
- **Levels 1–3** in `levels/`, generated by `tools/build-levels.mjs`, which draws designer paths through the real pen to make the easy and gold reference lines. Level 1 had to change (guide 682 → 527 px, marks 600/700, pot 800): its old numbers broke the 600 px trackpad rule. GDD updated.
- **Shell:** title (one-click PLAY, LEVELS, sound and music toggles; music on by default), level select (world tabs, sticky-note tiles with stars, ink % and gold nib, unlock rule "furthest finished + 2"), HUD (pause, level name, pen-shaped ink meter with ★3/★2 notches and % used, Try N, hint, restart; buttons take the full 80 px bar height), pause, results (stamped stars, ink % count-up, New best!, tries, Dev % and gold nib after 3★), saving (`cm_ink-path_save`, `cm_ink-path_settings`), synthesized SFX and a 96 BPM marimba loop with a shaker layer while Dot rolls, juice (squash, confetti, label pop, zoom punch, shake, speed trail, scratch particles). GA stub logs every GDD 11 event in the slice; per-action counts are sent once at level end. `window.game` adds `phase`, `paused`, `ink`, `attempts`, debug `win()` and `stroke(points)`.
- **Verified (Node only):** `node tools/simtest.mjs` passes 49/49: fall 0.842 s, quarter-pipe keeps 97–102% of ideal speed, a 10 px step rolls over and a 16 px one stops Dot, Dot rolls onto a stroke lying on the floor, a jittery 5° line rolls after RDP, each fail rule fires in time, determinism, pen blocking and dry ink, and for levels 1–3: fails with no line, easy wins shifted 0/±8 px, gold 0/±4 px, runs under 8 s, marks in order, easy lines ≤ 600 px. ## 2026-10-27 — Phase 2a build plan
Fafi said go ahead without waiting for a plan review, so this is the plan the slice is built from.
- **Files** (STANDARDS.md layout, GDD 12): `index.html`, `style.css`; `src/` = `main.js` (boot), `config.js` (every tuning number), `geom.js` (segment distance, point in polygon, RDP), `level.js` (level module → polygons, cup shapes, static segments, blocked test), `pen.js` (lazy string, spacing, blocked edges, ink, simplify; no DOM), `sim.js` (balls, contacts, cups, fail rules; no DOM, only + − × ÷ and sqrt), `game.js` (screens, attempt flow, HUD, analytics glue), `render.js`, `fx.js`, `input.js`, `audio.js`, `save.js`, `analytics.js`. `levels/` = one module per level plus `index.js`. `tools/` = `build-levels.mjs` (level geometry plus designer paths, recorded through the real pen into reference lines, until the phase 3 editor exists) and `simtest.mjs`.
- **Copied, not imported**, from Flip Runner: frame loop, resize and letterbox, the canvas button pattern matched by key on pointerup, panels, FX pool, Sound synth and sequencer, save and the GA stub (renamed and trimmed).
- **Order:** geometry and sim first, with `simtest.mjs` porting the scratch checks (fall time, curve keeps speed, 10 px lip, jittery 5° line, each fail rule, determinism) before any art; then the pen; then levels 1–3 and their reference lines tuned until the level rules pass; then the play screen; then the shell, audio and juice; last a Node smoke test of the whole game.
- **Faked or left out:** no grid broadphase (a slice level has under 200 segments, brute force with bounding boxes is enough); the wet ink is redrawn each frame instead of appended to a canvas; no "Watch best" replay, pens, title doodle or editor yet (the best line is already saved); no later-world elements.
- **Risks:** the contact rules (proved first in `simtest.mjs`); hand-drawn reference lines (built through the real pen and shift-tested); the trackpad feel (needs a real Chromebook, which the agent can't do).
